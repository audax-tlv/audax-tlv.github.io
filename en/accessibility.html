Accessibility statement

We want this site to be accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. It was built according to Israeli Standard 5568 and the WCAG 2.1 guidelines at level AA.

What we did

  • The whole site can be used with a keyboard only, including opening and closing videos (Esc closes).
  • A "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background.
  • Every button and image has a description for screen readers.
  • The videos at the top of the page play without sound and can be paused with a dedicated button. Visitors who set a reduced-motion preference on their device will not see them play automatically.
  • An accessibility menu (the round button in the bottom corner, or Alt+A) with adjustments: contrast, text size, line spacing, highlighted links and headings, a readable font, a big cursor and stopping animations. Your settings are saved in your browser for your next visit.
  • The moving logo strip stops when you hover over it and has a pause button.
  • The site works on phones, tablets and computers, and supports browser text zoom.

Known limitations

Some videos have burned-in English subtitles and no separate transcript. We are working on adding them.
